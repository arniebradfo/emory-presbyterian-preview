Welcome to

Emory Presbyterian Church

Always … Room for Everybody

Emory Presbyterian is a friendly, affirming congregation offering a place to serve and worship God and to support each other through prayer, faith exploration, and fellowship. We are engaged in building an inclusive community of faith, sharing God’s love and seeking to live out our faith as a Matthew 25 Church, actively engaged in the world.
